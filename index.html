04 — CULTURE

Traditions that live on.

Kerala's culture is a beautiful blend of art, music, dance, architecture, food and traditions passed from generation to generation.

PERFORMANCE

Kathakali

A dramatic classical art form known for elaborate costumes, expressions and storytelling.

CLASSICAL DANCE

Mohiniyattam

A graceful classical dance tradition deeply connected with Kerala's cultural heritage.

TRADITION

Theyyam

A powerful ritual performance tradition especially associated with northern Kerala.
