01 — THE LAND

MORE THAN A PLACE

Keralam is a feeling.

Between the Arabian Sea and the Western Ghats lies a land where nature and everyday life have always lived together.

Here, a morning can begin beside a quiet pond, continue through a coconut-lined village, cross a flowing river and end beneath the lights of a temple festival.

Keralam celebrates the simple things — rain falling on tiled roofs, the sound of oars touching water, the fragrance of spices, the rhythm of chenda and the warmth of people.

03 — CULTURE

LIVING HERITAGE

Traditions that breathe.

Kerala's culture lives through dance, music, architecture, food, rituals and stories passed from one generation to another.

01

Kathakali

A spectacular classical theatre tradition known for expressive eyes, elaborate costumes and powerful storytelling.

02

Theyyam

A powerful ritual performance deeply connected with the cultural traditions of northern Kerala.

03

Nalukettu

Traditional Kerala homes built around a central courtyard, reflecting climate, architecture and family life.

04 — FESTIVALS

When the whole land celebrates.

Festivals transform villages, streets and temple grounds into places of music, colour, flowers and community.

01

Onam

The harvest festival celebrated with pookkalam, sadya, traditional games and the spirit of togetherness.

02

Thrissur Pooram

A spectacular celebration of percussion, decorated elephants, umbrellas and community spirit.

03

Vallam Kali

Traditional boat races where long snake boats glide through Kerala's waterways to the rhythm of songs and oars.
